Nonprofits

Less time on paperwork. More time on the mission.

Small teams lose weeks to grant research and applications. We find the right funders, write the applications, and build the program materials behind them.

Funding and programs

Four services, used alone or together.

Grant discovery

We search federal, state, foundation, and corporate funding and hand you a short list of grants that fit your mission, size, and location, with deadlines.

  • Fit check
  • Deadline calendar
  • Funder research

Grant writing

Narratives, budgets, and supporting sections in your organization’s voice. AI produces the first draft. A person edits it and checks it against the funder’s rules before you sign.

  • Full applications
  • Budgets and narratives
  • Reviewed before you sign

Donor and foundation relationships

Funders give to organizations they know. We research who supports work like yours nearby, prep you for introductions and meetings, and draft thank-yous and progress updates. A person tracks follow-ups so funders hear from you between applications.

  • Local funder research
  • Meeting prep
  • Thank-yous and updates

Program curriculum

Lesson plans, activities, and facilitator guides behind your programs, including youth programs, so what you promise a funder matches what you teach.

  • Youth and program curricula
  • Facilitator guides
  • Age and accuracy checked
