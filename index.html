How the work actually gets done

Three steps. AI handles the typing and sorting so the people on your project can spend their time on judgment.

  2. 1

    Brief

    You tell us the goal, the budget, and what is off limits. We write it up as a plan you can read and correct before any work starts.

    AISummarizes what you send and researches your market.
    A personFixes what is wrong and signs off with you.

    Posts, ad copy, keyword lists, grant language, rough cuts. First drafts come fast. Good drafts take a person.
