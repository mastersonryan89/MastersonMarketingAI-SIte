How the AI team works

03 / 07   Process

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell us the goal, the budget, and what is off limits. We turn that into a written plan you can read and correct before any work starts.

    AI doesSummarizes what you send us and researches your market and competitors.
    A person doesReads the plan, fixes what is wrong, and signs off with you.
  2. 02

    Build

    Ad copy, keyword lists, grant language, rough cuts. This is where the AI does the bulk of the typing and sorting, so the humans can spend time on judgment.

    AI doesProduces first drafts and options, fast.
    A person doesRewrites, fact-checks, and throws out whatever is not good enough.
  3. 03

    Ship

    We launch, then keep watching. Campaigns get adjusted, applications get submitted, and finished video and audio get delivered.

    AI doesFlags changes in performance and drafts report summaries.
    A person doesReviews everything before it goes live and reads every report before you see it.

AI is a tool we use, not a replacement for judgment. A person reviews all work before it reaches you or goes public.
