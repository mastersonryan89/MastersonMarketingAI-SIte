Who you're actually hiring

I'm Ryan Masterson. I started Masterson Marketing & AI Solutions in Columbus in 2025 to do this work directly for the kind of organizations that can't justify a full marketing hire.

The model

I work inside a small number of Columbus organizations every week, businesses and nonprofits, running the marketing and communications they'd otherwise need three hires to cover.

Small businesses and mission-driven organizations need different things and pay for them differently, so I keep the two separate. What doesn't change is who shows up: no account manager, no handoff to a junior, no agency in between.

The AI in the name is how one person covers that much ground. I use it to research, draft, and handle the repetitive parts faster, so more of your hours go to the work that needs judgment.

The constraint

I take on a small number of clients because the arrangement only works if I'm genuinely in your business every week. When you call, you get me. When something ships, I built it.

That's the whole model, and it's why I'd rather have four clients I know well than forty I don't.
