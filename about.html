About

A small Columbus team that does the work of a big one.

Masterson Marketing & AI Solutions LLC is a marketing company for local businesses and nonprofits. AI handles the busywork. People handle the judgment.

From the founder

You talk to the person doing the work.

I started Masterson Marketing so businesses and nonprofits in central Ohio could get a full marketing team without paying for one. AI lets a small team move fast. It does not get to decide what goes out under your name. A person does that, every time.

If you call, you get me. If you email, I read it. And if what you need is not something we do well, I will tell you who does.
