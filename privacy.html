Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, business name, email address, phone number, and anything else you enter into a form on this site or tell us directly.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide the services you asked about.

SMS and text messaging

Text messaging consent By giving us your mobile number — on a form, by text, or by asking us to contact you — you agree to receive text messages from Masterson Marketing & AI Solutions related to your inquiry, appointments, and the services you asked about.

  • Message frequency varies depending on your conversation with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard charges apply to messages you send and receive.
  • To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any message at any time. You will get one confirmation message and no further messages after that.

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or your consent to receive text messages with any third party for their own marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Services we provide to our clients

We build and manage automated messaging and appointment systems on behalf of our business clients. When we do that work, our client — the business you contacted — is responsible for obtaining consent from its own customers, and its own privacy policy governs that relationship. We act as a service provider to that business and use the information only to deliver the service.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate — for example, telephone and messaging carriers, email providers, scheduling and CRM software, and hosting providers — and only as needed to do their job.

Cookies

This site may use cookies or similar technology to keep the site working properly and understand general traffic patterns. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it unidentifiable.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold, including access controls and reputable third-party platforms. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices
